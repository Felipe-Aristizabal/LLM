IFC invests in Tecnoquímicas

2008-07-08 16:15:59
08 de julio de 2008
bookmark Guardar

With an investment of 25 million dollars, the International Finance Corporation of the World Bank Club joined the expansion project of the company Tecnoquímicas, a pharmaceutical company in Cali, Valle del Cauca.

Tecnoquímicas has nine plants in Colombia already and a venue in Ecuador and wants to expand itself to other departments in the country.

Recently Tecnoquímicas announced a budget of 100 million dollars aimed at opening new factories in the Andes and Central America.
